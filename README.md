### Free tools

Small checks for problems I'm researching, built with AI agents. No sign-up; most run in your browser and upload nothing.

**Shopify and e-commerce**
- [Books Sync Check](https://books-sync-check.pages.dev/check/): Shopify orders your QuickBooks or Xero connector missed, doubled or got wrong
- [Store Break Check](https://store-break-check.pages.dev/check/): cart, checkout, search and links on a Shopify store
- [Misrepresentation Pre-check](https://misrep-precheck.pages.dev/check/): what Google Merchant Center may flag as misrepresentation
- [Product Feed Check](https://product-feed-check.pages.dev/check/): products Google and Meta will reject
- [True Weekly Profit](https://true-weekly-profit.pages.dev/calc/): profit per SKU after every marketplace fee
- [Order Sync Watch](https://ordersync-watch.pages.dev/compare/): orders missing between a shop and Royal Mail Click & Drop
- [List Cost Check](https://list-cost-check.pages.dev/check/): Klaviyo profiles you pay for that will never buy

**IT, email and security**
- [DMARC Sender Check](https://dmarc-sender-check.pages.dev/check/): every service sending as your domain, and which ones fail DKIM or SPF
- [Cert Renewal Forecast](https://cert-renewal-forecast.pages.dev/forecast/): certificates you renew by hand, before the 2027 lifetime cuts
- [extcheck](https://extcheck.pages.dev): VS Code extension ownership changes and risk
- [Bank Change Check](https://bank-change-check.pages.dev/check/): red flags in a supplier's bank-change email
- [MSP Takeover Checklist](https://msp-takeover-checklist.pages.dev/checklist/): a takeover checklist pre-filled from a client's domain
- [A2P Rejection Decoder](https://a2p-rejection-decoder.pages.dev/decode/): a 10DLC rejection code turned into the fix

**Accessibility**
- [Title II PDF Check](https://title-ii-pdf-check.pages.dev/check/): how many of a site's PDFs fail a machine check
- [Plain Access](https://plainaccess.pages.dev/check/): accessibility fixes for WordPress, not an overlay
- [ACR Freshness Check](https://acr-freshness-check.pages.dev/check/): whether a VPAT/ACR is current for WCAG 2.2

**Developer and ops**
- [Burnmeter](https://burnmeter.pages.dev): AI-coding spend per day, before the invoice
- [Prompt Regression Check](https://prompt-regression-check.pages.dev/evals-export/): export OpenAI Evals before they go read-only
- [Selectorwatch](https://selectorwatch.pages.dev/check/): scraper selectors that broke when the page changed
- [Site Fleet Health](https://site-fleet-health.pages.dev/check/): which client WordPress sites updated, backed up and stayed up
- [On-call Rota Planner](https://oncall-rota-planner.pages.dev/plan/): a fair on-call rota with holidays and time zones
- [Task Meter](https://task-meter.pages.dev/estimate/): which Zaps eat your Zapier task budget
- [Ratecard Invoices](https://ratecard-invoices.pages.dev/invoice/): Jira worklogs into invoices with the right rates
- [Test Case Export](https://testcase-export.pages.dev/compare/): getting test cases out of Zephyr or Xray
