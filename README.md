### Free tools

Small checks for problems I'm researching, built with AI agents. No sign-up; most run in your browser and upload nothing. All of them: [opsfixes.com](https://opsfixes.com).

**Shopify and e-commerce**
- [Books Sync Check](https://opsfixes.com/books-sync-check/check/): Shopify orders your QuickBooks or Xero connector missed, doubled or got wrong
- [Store Break Check](https://opsfixes.com/store-break-check/check/): cart, checkout, search and links on a Shopify store
- [Misrepresentation Pre-check](https://opsfixes.com/misrep-precheck/check/): what Google Merchant Center may flag as misrepresentation
- [Product Feed Check](https://opsfixes.com/product-feed-check/check/): products Google and Meta will reject
- [True Weekly Profit](https://opsfixes.com/true-weekly-profit/calc/): profit per SKU after every marketplace fee
- [Order Sync Watch](https://opsfixes.com/ordersync-watch/compare/): orders missing between a shop and Royal Mail Click & Drop
- [List Cost Check](https://opsfixes.com/list-cost-check/check/): Klaviyo profiles you pay for that will never buy
